LabelGuard is an automated compliance intelligence and packaging verification system designed for regulatory enforcement officers, quality assurance teams, and consumer goods auditors. It streamlines the inspection of packaged commodities by detecting mandatory declarations, assessing regulatory conformance, and calculating audit scores in real time.

🔗 Live Application: https://baintanay007.github.io/LabelGuard/

📌 Key Highlights
Automated Label Inspection: Drag-and-drop packaging photos or labels (PNG, JPG, WEBP) to run automated computer vision & OCR-based declaration audits.

Regulatory Declarations Verification:

✅ Manufacturer / Packer Name & Physical Address

✅ Net Quantity & Standard Unit of Measure

✅ MRP / Unit Sale Price (USP)

✅ Manufacturing Date & Expiry / Best Before details

✅ Consumer Care / Grievance Redressal Information

✅ Ingredients & Nutritional Information panel compliance

Scoring & Risk Assessment Engine: Computes an automated compliance index (0–100) and categorizes products as Compliant, Needs Review, or Non-Compliant.

Enforcement Workspace & Dashboard: Comprehensive audit tracking with time-series inspection activity charts, distribution breakdowns, and detailed product audit logs.
